Model controls and assumptions

Variants: baseline, stronger-hints, p5-p6-clue-relief, meta-prop-clarity, team-parallelism, guided-final-set. P5/P6 alone receive clue relief. Solve times are sorted then chunked by parallelism, not optimally scheduled. Five feeder answers are assumed available for the meta; no answer/extraction is checked. Hint thresholds count pressure and do not reduce solve minutes. The target is 180 minutes.
